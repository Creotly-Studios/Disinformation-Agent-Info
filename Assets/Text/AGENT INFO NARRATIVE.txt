### **LEVEL 1 Dialogue**

**Setting:**  
A dimly lit room. The player can walk around and explore briefly, but when they interact with Ms. Maggie, the dialogue begins.

**[START]**  
**Ms. Maggie:** _"Ah, Mr. Wade Kim, welcome to the I.M.A."_

**Player:** _"Hey... sup?"_

**Ms. Maggie:** _"By now, I assume you’ve been briefed?"_

**Player:**

- _"Not really."_
- _"Sort of... I guess?"_

**Ms. Maggie:**  
_"Well then, let me fill in the blanks. You’ve been drafted into the Information Monitoring and Managing Agency — the I.M.A. We’re a global organization tasked with curbing the spread of disinformation. Our mission began centuries ago, around the time newspapers were first printed. Back then, it was simpler... now, the battlefield is the digital world."_

**Player:** _"That’s cool and all, but what exactly am I here for?"_

**Ms. Maggie:** _"Hmph, straight to the point. I like that. To ease you into things, you’ll be starting in our simulator. Think of it as a hands-on introduction to the kind of missions we handle daily."_

**Player:** _"Alright, let’s see what you’ve got. Hit me."_

**Ms. Maggie:**  
_"Good. Head through that door over there and begin the simulation. Prove yourself, Agent."_

**[END]**

---

**Gameplay:**  
The player completes the tutorial simulation, set in a sterile white room with holographic robots and prototype textures.

---

**[START]**  
**Ms. Maggie:** _"Impressive, Agent Kim. You scored a solid {insert player score here}. That’s better than most recruits."_

**Player:** _"Not bad, huh? Thanks, boss."_

**Ms. Maggie:**  
_"You’ve earned your first official mission. From this point forward, you’ll be operating in the field. Here’s your teleportation band. You’ll use it to travel to and from mission sites via specially marked phone booths."_

**Player:** _"Got it. So, where am I headed first?"_

**Ms. Maggie:**  
_"Your first assignment takes you to the town of Yonkle Storntle. It’s election season there, and one of the main candidates is spreading disinformation about their opponents. Your job is to stop the spread before it escalates into chaos. Be vigilant — bots are likely in play, stirring the pot. Neutralize them if needed."_

**Player:** _"Understood. Yonkle Storntle, bots, disinformation. I’m on it."_

**Ms. Maggie:** _"Good. The phone booth is waiting for you. Don’t let us down."_

**[END]**

---

**Gameplay:**  
The player interacts with the phone booth, activates the teleportation band, and is transported to Yonkle Storntle for their first mission.
